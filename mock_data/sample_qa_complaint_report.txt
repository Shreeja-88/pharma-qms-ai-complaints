2. COMPLAINT DETAILS & DEFECT DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During routine incoming batch inspection at the hospital dispensary, quality 
control auditors observed physical abnormalities in Batch AMX260588B.

Observed Defect:
Multiple sealed glass bottles exhibited clumping and severe caking of the powder 
mix prior to reconstitution. Upon inspection, 15% of tested bottles showed 
discoloration (darkening to brown tint). The primary foil induction seals on 
the affected bottles appeared loose or incompletely torqued during capping.

Impact Assessment:
Potential moisture ingress leading to active ingredient degradation and loss of 
potency. Immediate quarantine of the entire lot at hospital level was executed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INITIAL QA ACTION REQUESTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Issue immediate Batch Quarantine Notice for AMX260588B across all centers.
- Initiate Root Cause Analysis (RCA) regarding capping torque / sealing line 3.
- Authorize immediate replacement of 120 affected cartons to maintain supply.
